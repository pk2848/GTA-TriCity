# Chandigarh landmark reference notes

The game uses public images and official descriptions as **visual research references**. These images are not downloaded or embedded as game textures in this release; the in-game geometry is original Three.js work. The OSM roads, ordinary campus footprints and Sukhna water polygon remain OpenStreetMap-derived and require ODbL attribution.

## Accuracy and licensing notes

- Campus building dimensions and some South Campus/shoreline scenic placements are approximate. The custom modules are not surveyed architectural models or official CAD/BIM reconstructions.
- The Sukhna surface outline is taken from OpenStreetMap data. Do not replace the water body with a guessed circle/ellipse.
- Images listed here are research references; no third-party photograph is packaged as a material or texture in this release.
- Preserve OpenStreetMap attribution and comply with ODbL when redistributing the map-derived geometry.
